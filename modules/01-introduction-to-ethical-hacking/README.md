# Module 01 — Introduction to Ethical Hacking

## Overview
Foundational concepts of information security, the Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATT&CK framework, ethical hacking phases, and the legal/regulatory landscape governing authorized security testing.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the Information Security triad (CIA) and attack surface concepts
- Differentiate between hacking phases: Reconnaissance, Scanning, Gaining Access, Maintaining Access, Covering Tracks
- Understand hacker classifications (White/Grey/Black hat) and threat actor motivations
- Map attacker behavior to MITRE ATT&CK and the Cyber Kill Chain
- Understand the legal scope of engagement: NDAs, Rules of Engagement (RoE), and authorization requirements

## Detection & Mitigation
- Establish a formal security policy aligned to ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST CSF
- Define scope, rules of engagement, and legal authorization (signed contract) before any assessment
- Adopt a risk-based vulnerability management program with defined SLAs

## Key Definitions
| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CIA Triad** | Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability — the three core objectives of information security. |
| **Threat** | Any circumstance/event with potential to adversely impact an asset. |
| **Vulnerability** | A weakness that can be exploited by a threat to cause harm. |
| **Risk** | The likelihood of a threat exploiting a vulnerability multiplied by resulting impact. |
| **Rules of Engagement (RoE)** | A formal document defining scope, timing, and constraints of an authorized penetration test. |
